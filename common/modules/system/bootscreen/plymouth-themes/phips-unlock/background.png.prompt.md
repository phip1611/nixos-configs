# Image Description / Regeneration Prompt

Create a wide, low-height cyberpunk boot-logo graphic on a pure black
background. The image should feel futuristic, dystopian, technical, slightly
dirty and imperfect rather than polished or corporate. It should combine bold
typography, rainbow color gradients, dripping paint, electronic circuit-board
patterns, microchip imagery, and small HUD/interface details.

## Overall composition

- Very wide horizontal format, approximately 2:1 aspect ratio.
- Pure black background (`#000000`) with no visible texture at the outer edges.
- All visual elements are concentrated in a relatively narrow horizontal band
  through the center of the image.
- The artwork should fade naturally and seamlessly into complete black toward:
  - the top,
  - the bottom,
  - the far left,
  - and the far right.
- Preserve substantial black negative space around the graphic so that it works
  well as a Plymouth boot logo placed over a black screen.
- The composition is roughly symmetrical in visual weight, but not geometrically
  symmetrical.
- The main word occupies most of the width.
- Supporting illustrations and small technical labels sit around the main text
  rather than competing with it.

## Main text

The central text must read exactly:

**Phips**

- Very large.
- Bold.
- Angular, italicized, futuristic techno / cyberpunk lettering.
- Strong forward-slanted appearance, suggesting speed and movement.
- Thick geometric strokes.
- Some cuts, notches, angular corners and segmented shapes inside the letters.
- The word should dominate the image.
- The typography should resemble a combination of:
  - futuristic motorsport typography,
  - cyberpunk HUD graphics,
  - industrial stencil design,
  - angular sci-fi branding.
- Avoid rounded, friendly, playful or handwritten fonts.
- Do not make the text perfectly clean.

### Surface treatment of "Phips"

The letters should look physically distressed:

- scratched,
- chipped,
- cracked,
- worn,
- uneven,
- partially splattered,
- with tiny missing fragments,
- and irregular black scratches cutting into the colored surfaces.

The texture should make it look like painted metal, damaged signage, or a worn
futuristic machine panel.

## Rainbow gradient

Apply a continuous rainbow-like gradient across the word from left to right.

Suggested progression:

- far left of **P**: hot magenta / red,
- then red-orange,
- orange,
- yellow,
- yellow-green,
- green,
- turquoise,
- cyan,
- bright blue toward the right side of the final **s**.

The colors should be vivid but not pastel.

The gradient should:

- feel luminous,
- remain highly saturated,
- blend smoothly between colors,
- retain some local imperfections,
- and contrast strongly against the black background.

Avoid a clean "Pride flag stripe" appearance. It should look like one continuous
luminous cyberpunk spectrum.

## Paint drips and gravity

Underneath the letters, add downward-running paint drips.

Important:

- All drips, splashes, liquid streaks and falling fragments obey gravity.
- They only extend downward from the letters and nearby graphic elements.
- Do not create upward splashes.
- Do not create large radial explosions.
- Do not create splashes shooting sideways or upward.

The dripping should be:

- irregular,
- thin in most places,
- occasionally long,
- with some droplets hanging at the ends,
- more prominent below the lower edges of the letters.

Some drips should extend noticeably farther than others, especially beneath the
central and right-hand letters.

Colors of the drips should correspond to the local color of the rainbow gradient
above them.

Example:

- red/magenta drips below the left side,
- yellow drips under the center-left,
- green drips near the center,
- cyan/blue drips under the right side.

## Circuit-board background

Behind and around the word, integrate a network of thin electronic circuit-board
traces.

These traces should:

- appear mostly behind the text,
- extend horizontally outward from the main logo,
- follow geometric 45-degree and 90-degree paths,
- use small round connection pads,
- contain small nodes, vias and terminal circles,
- resemble PCB traces or motherboard routing.

Color treatment:

- left side: dark red / orange,
- center: dark yellow / green,
- right side: teal / cyan.

The circuits should be much darker than the main text.

They should not overpower the logo.

They should fade into black as they approach the edges of the image.

The circuit patterns should be dense immediately around the word and become
increasingly sparse farther away.

## Right-side microchip

In the upper-right portion of the composition, include a stylized square CPU /
microchip.

It should be:

- dark,
- thinly outlined in cyan / teal,
- surrounded by many small pins,
- integrated into the circuit-board traces,
- approximately square,
- technical rather than photorealistic.

Inside the chip, place subtle stacked text:

**PHIPS**
**CORE**
**10.0**

The text should be small and dim.

The chip should look like part of the circuitry rather than a floating icon.

Circuit traces should visibly connect to its pins.

## Left-side text block

Below the left side of the main word, place the text:

**IN RUST WE TRUST**

- All capitals.
- Monospaced or technical terminal-style typeface.
- Red / orange-red.
- Small compared with the main logo.
- Aligned horizontally.
- Slightly detached from the main word but clearly part of the design.

Directly underneath it, include several small technical / warning icons in a
horizontal row.

Possible icons:

- outlined square,
- warning triangle,
- abstract hashed square,
- diagonal industrial hatching,
- simple geometric diagnostic symbols.

The icons should look like industrial control-panel graphics.

## Secure Boot element

In the lower-left area, beneath the warning icons, include:

**SECURE BOOT**

- All capitals.
- Monospaced technical font.
- Red / orange-red.
- Relatively small.

Add a small padlock icon immediately to the left of the words.

The padlock should:

- be an outline,
- use the same red color,
- look like a minimal HUD / terminal symbol.

Below or near "SECURE BOOT", optionally include:

- a thin diagnostic progress bar,
- short hash marks,
- small indicator lines,
- very subtle red interface decoration.

Keep this area sparse and technical.

## Right-side NixOS element

Below the right side of the main logo, add:

**NIXOS**

- All capitals.
- Cyan / teal.
- Monospaced or futuristic technical font.
- Larger than the other diagnostic labels but still much smaller than "Phips".

Under the word "NIXOS", include:

- a horizontal barcode-like graphic,
- tiny technical text,
- short diagnostic line patterns.

To the right of this area, include:

**v2.1.7**

- very small,
- cyan / teal,
- monospaced.

Also include a small square QR-code-like graphic.

The QR-like graphic should look decorative and technical; it does not need to
encode meaningful information.

## Additional hardware details

Subtly incorporate illustrations inspired by:

- motherboard traces,
- CPU pins,
- silicon dies,
- IC packages,
- microcontroller layouts,
- bus routes,
- tiny chip components,
- connector pads,
- diagnostic test points.

These should appear as technical background elements rather than literal
photographs.

Avoid large recognizable consumer hardware such as:

- complete keyboards,
- monitors,
- laptops,
- gaming PCs,
- mice.

The emphasis should be on low-level computer hardware and silicon / PCB design.

## Cyberpunk atmosphere

The visual identity should combine:

- cyberpunk,
- low-level computing,
- hardware hacking,
- industrial electronics,
- dystopian technology,
- damaged futuristic infrastructure,
- underground hacker aesthetics.

It should feel:

- technical,
- brave,
- slightly aggressive,
- artistic,
- unconventional,
- dirty,
- imperfect,
- modern,
- lightweight rather than overloaded.

Avoid glossy "corporate cyberpunk".

Avoid excessive 3D chrome.

Avoid generic neon-city imagery.

Avoid human figures.

Avoid buildings or city skylines.

Avoid anime characters.

Avoid spaceships.

The image should remain fundamentally a logo / boot-screen graphic, not an
illustration or scene.

## Imperfection and dystopian details

Introduce controlled visual damage:

- tiny paint flecks,
- scratches,
- chipped edges,
- interrupted PCB traces,
- partial circuit lines,
- broken segments,
- tiny glitches,
- irregular opacity,
- small black holes inside the paint,
- subtle asymmetry.

The damage should look deliberate and artistic.

Do not make the image noisy everywhere.

Maintain strong areas of pure black.

## Lighting

- No conventional light source.
- The colored elements themselves appear faintly emissive.
- Very subtle neon glow around some colored edges.
- Glow should remain restrained.
- Do not create large halos.
- Black should stay truly black away from the logo.
- No gray background haze.

## Vertical proportions

The logo must remain compact in height.

- Main letters occupy most of the usable height.
- Supporting circuitry should extend only slightly above them.
- Drips can extend somewhat farther downward, but should not make the graphic
excessively tall.
- Overall artwork height should be roughly 1.3-1.6× the height of the letters.
- Do not create tall towers, vertical city structures or huge decorative
elements.
- Keep the artwork visually wide and flat.

## Edge behavior

This is important for a Plymouth boot screen.

At the boundaries of the artwork:

- all circuitry,
- paint,
- glow,
- particles,
- splashes,
- and details

must fade smoothly into pure black.

There should be no:

- rectangular border,
- visible canvas edge,
- frame,
- vignette outline,
- background gradient that remains visible at the outer edge.

The artwork should look as though it naturally emerges from and disappears into
a completely black screen.

## Priority order

The most visually important elements should be:

1. The word **Phips**
2. Rainbow gradient
3. Distressed / dripping paint treatment
4. Circuit-board and hardware motif
5. **IN RUST WE TRUST**
6. **NIXOS**
7. **SECURE BOOT**
8. CPU marked **PHIPS CORE 10.0**
9. Small barcode / QR / diagnostic details

## Desired final impression

A personal hacker / low-level systems engineering boot logo: "Phips" rendered as
a damaged rainbow cyberpunk hardware emblem, surrounded by subtle motherboard
circuitry and low-level computing references. It should feel like the boot
identity of a custom-built dystopian operating system: artistic and colorful,
but technical, dark, imperfect, minimal enough to work as a real boot screen,
and seamlessly embedded into a pure black background.
